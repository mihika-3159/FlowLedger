# 🎙️ FlowLedger Demo Script

Use this script for a 3-5 minute presentation of the FlowLedger platform.

## 1. The Hook (30 Seconds)
"Most companies are losing 20-30% of their productivity to invisible operational waste—manual repetition, slow approvals, and redundant work. But you can't fix what you can't measure. Meet **FlowLedger**, the ROI-driven audit platform that turns messy workflows into precise automation roadmaps."

## 2. The Dashboard (1 Minute)
*Navigation: Home (/) -> Click "Load Demo Data"*
- "We're looking at a global view of operational health. Here we see that across our 5 core workflows, we're wasting over 100 hours a week."
- "The **Waste Breakdown** shows us exactly where it's happening—mostly in manual repetition and approval delays."
- "But the most important metric is at the bottom: **Quick Wins**. We've already identified $320k in annual savings that can be realized with low-effort automation."

## 3. The Deep Dive (1 Minute)
*Navigation: Workflows -> Select "New Employee Onboarding" -> Click "Run Efficiency Audit"*
- "Let's take a closer look at Onboarding. It feels slow, but why? Our AI engine is now auditing every step of the process."
- "The analysis identifies a P0 bottleneck: 'Hardware Provisioning Delay'. It's causing 15 hours of waste per week."
- "We can use the **ROI Calculator** to show stakeholders that by automating just this one step, we save $45,000 every single year."

## 4. Visualization & Action (1 Minute)
*Navigation: Click "View Flow Map"*
- "We don't just give you a report; we give you a map. The red edges here highlight where the handoffs are failing."
- "Finally, we turn this insight into action. Click **Build Action Plan**."
- "FlowLedger generates a staged roadmap. Phase 1 focuses on the P0 quick wins. As we check off these items, the system tracks our realized ROI in real-time."

## 5. Closing (15 Seconds)
"FlowLedger doesn't just find problems—it builds the business case for solving them. It's the ledger for your company's most valuable asset: its time. Thank you."
